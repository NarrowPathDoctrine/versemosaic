VerseMosaic

Privacy Policy

VerseMosaic is a King James Bible study app created by Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Overview

VerseMosaic lets you read Scripture, follow cross-references, explore commentary and word-study resources, save verses, and create personal notes. The app is designed primarily around information stored on your Android device. You do not need to create a VerseMosaic account to use it.

Information stored on your device

VerseMosaic may store personal study information locally so the features you choose to use can work. This includes saved Scripture, personal notes, reading and exploration progress, and app settings such as appearance and reminder preferences.

The app does not operate a VerseMosaic user account or cloud-sync service for this information. Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries does not maintain a server containing your personal notes, bookmarks, or Scripture reading history.

Scripture and study resources

The app displays King James Bible text, curated commentary, Scripture connections, and related study resources. Your Scripture reading, verse selections, and personal notes are processed as part of the app's functions on your device. VerseMosaic does not require you to send your reading history or notes to Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries.

Daily Discovery notifications

If you enable a Daily Discovery reminder, VerseMosaic uses Android notifications to remind you to open the day's Scripture content. Reminder preferences are stored on your device. You can turn reminders off in the app or manage notification permissions in Android settings.

Sharing and external services

Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries does not sell your VerseMosaic notes, saved Scripture, or reading history to advertisers or data brokers. If you choose to copy or share content with another app, the destination you select handles that information according to its own practices.

When you follow an external link, the website or service you open may receive standard information associated with an Internet request, such as your IP address, and may have its own privacy policy. Android, Google Play, and device or backup providers may also process technical information through their separate services. Those activities are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Data security

VerseMosaic relies on Android's app-storage protections for locally held study information. We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping its software updated. No storage technology can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

Study information saved by VerseMosaic generally remains on your device until you change or remove it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Android's system settings let you clear VerseMosaic's stored data. Because the app does not provide user accounts, there is no VerseMosaic account to close.

Depending on your device's backup configuration, Android or a backup provider may retain copies of some app information. These separate backups are subject to the provider's own settings and retention practices.

Children's privacy

VerseMosaic is a Scripture study app and does not require children or other users to register an account. Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a VerseMosaic account or developer-operated study-data service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to VerseMosaic's features or data practices. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, use the developer contact information provided on VerseMosaic's Google Play listing.
